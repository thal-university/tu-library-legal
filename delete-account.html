Privacy PolicyDelete my account

Option 1 – Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Log in to the TU Library App.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 – Request deletion by email (no app needed)

If you can no longer open the app, email library@tu.edu.pk with the subject “Delete my TU Library App account”. Please send it from your official university email and include your roll number (students) so we can verify it is you. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

Before you delete

  • You must have no unreturned books and no unpaid fines. If you do, deletion is not possible until they are cleared at the library desk. After that, you can delete right away.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You can create a new account later using your roll number.

What is deleted

  • Your login account and password
  • Your profile used for the app and Digital ID
  • Your in-app notifications, waitlist entries and push notification token
  • Any pending (not yet issued) reservation requests are cancelled

What we keep, and why

As a university library we keep an official record for audit, accounting and dispute resolution: your name, roll number/email, CNIC, department, and your book reservation, issue, return and fine history. This record is stored securely, cannot be accessed from the app, is used only for official library/university purposes, and is not shared outside the university except where required by law. See Privacy Policy – Data retention.
