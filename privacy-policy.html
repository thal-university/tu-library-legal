1. Who we are

The TU Library App ("the app") is the mobile app of the library of Thal University Bhakkar ("the Library", "we"). It lets students and faculty browse books, reserve books, view due dates and fines, receive notifications and show a digital library ID.

Contact: library@tu.edu.pk

2. Information we collect

Guests (people who tap “Continue as Guest”) can browse the book catalogue and library information. No account is created and we do not collect personal information from guests.

Account holders (students and faculty who sign up):

DataWhy we need it
Roll number (students) or official university email (faculty)To verify you against university records and identify your account
Full name, father’s name, CNIC, department, role/designationObtained from university records to verify who you are and show your Digital ID. Your CNIC is shown masked in the app and is never shown publicly.
Official email addressTo send sign-up / device-verification / password-reset codes
PasswordHandled and stored (hashed) by our authentication provider. We never see or store your plain password.
Device identifier (Android ID or an app-generated ID)To allow only one active device per account (account security). Not used for advertising.
Push notification tokenTo send you book-available, due-date and fine notifications
Reservation records: books reserved/issued/returned, dates, due dates, fines and whether paid; waitlist entries; in-app notificationsTo run the library service and to calculate and record fines

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and let you log in securely
  • To process reservations, issue and return books, and record fines
  • To send service notifications (availability, reminders, fines)
  • To protect accounts (single-device login, one-time codes)
  • To keep official library records as required by the university

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Your information is not shared with other users or the public. It is processed on our behalf by these service providers:

  • Supabase – database and authentication hosting (data is encrypted in transit and stored on servers in India, Mumbai region).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Thal University student/faculty records – your roll number or email is checked against university records to verify you.

Library staff of Thal University can see reservation, issue, return and fine records in order to run the library. We may disclose information if required by law or by the university’s administration.

5. Data retention and what we keep after account deletion

You can delete your account at any time (see how to delete your account). When you do, your login, profile, notification history, push token and waitlist entries are deleted.

Retained for official use: because the library lends university property and may charge fines, we keep a record of your name, roll number/email, CNIC, department and your reservation, issue, return and fine history. We keep this for official library and university audit, accounting and dispute-resolution purposes. It is stored securely, is not accessible from the app, is not used for any other purpose and is not shared outside the university except where required by law.

For the same reason, an account cannot be deleted while you still hold an unreturned book or have an unpaid fine. Once these are cleared, deletion is available immediately.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to your data is restricted by database access rules so each user can only see their own records, and passwords are stored hashed by the authentication provider. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • View your profile and reservation history in the app
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete Account, or by emailing us (see delete my account)
  • Turn off notifications in your phone’s settings
  • Ask us to correct wrong information, or ask a question about your data: library@tu.edu.pk

8. Children

The app is for university students and staff and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify users in the app.
